Getting started

Four short steps: flash the firmware, drop one file on the SD card, turn on the device's Bluetooth, and connect.

Sync

Hold the power button on the X4 for about two seconds, until the screen says Bluetooth is on. Then connect and sync. A short hold matters — the automatic window after each face change is only seconds long, which is rarely enough to finish pairing.
