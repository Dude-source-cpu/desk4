Send your photos and content

Hold the power button on the frame for two seconds, until it says Bluetooth is on. Then send.

Why do I have to hold the button?

Bluetooth is the most power-hungry thing in the frame, so it stays off. The frame does switch it on briefly after every screen change, but only for a few seconds — enough for this page to reconnect once paired, rarely enough to pair the first time. Holding the button gives you five full minutes.

While the frame is plugged in to USB it stays awake and reachable the whole time.
