First-time setup

The X4 only becomes a photo frame — and only turns its Bluetooth on — once /photox4/config.json exists on the SD card, so that one file has to arrive over USB. Everything after it comes over Bluetooth.

  1. Flash the companion firmware build to the device.
  2. Download config.json below and copy it into a photox4 folder at the root of the SD card.
  3. Put the card back. The device shows its first face at the next wake.
  4. Hold the power button until it says Bluetooth is on, then press Connect above.

Sync

Long-press the power button on the X4 until it says Bluetooth is on, then press Sync. If auto-sync is enabled the device also opens a short Bluetooth window every time it changes face, and this page will connect to it on its own while the tab stays open.

Rotation

A short click of the power button skips to the next face at any time. Every wake costs a little battery, so a shorter interval and a longer Bluetooth window both shorten the time between charges.
